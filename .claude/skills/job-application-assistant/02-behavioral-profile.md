## Overview
Jacob's profile is best described as a **curious systems-thinker who specializes through depth rather than breadth once he commits**. A decade of genuinely varied, curiosity-driven work (climbing instruction, TV production, arboriculture, air conditioning, alongside a family sign business) preceded a deliberate 2023 decision to stop being a generalist and specialize in UX design — the pattern is exploration first, then real commitment, not restlessness.
